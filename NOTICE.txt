The adapted files assets/delta-graph.js and the Delta-derived portions of assets/connections.js and assets/connections.css are distributed under GPLv3 or later as part of this plugin. Changes isolate the renderer, scope styles, adapt data to Etch and WordPress, and integrate selection and editing with the plugin panel.
