# Meet Content Explorer for Etch 2.0

## Find your content. See how it connects.

Etch Content Hub Sort is now Content Explorer for Etch. Version 2.0 brings your content lists and visual connection maps into one dedicated panel inside the builder.

Find a page, follow its links, see the shared resources it uses, or explore the structure of your site without losing the document you're editing.

### A list built for everyday work

Search and sort across posts, pages, custom content types, and taxonomies. Browse parent and child items, choose which columns to display, and keep Quick edit and Connections in a consistent place beside each title.

Create content, update its details, manage workflow statuses, or jump into Etch or WordPress when you need the full editor. A collapsible, resizable menu gives you more room to work.

### See the connections behind your content

Open an item's map to explore incoming links, outgoing links, templates, components, and other saved shared resources. Select a card to see its details and available editing options. Shared-resource maps help you find the content that uses them before you make a change.

### Take a wider view

The Site map brings public content types, entries, and taxonomy relationships into an interactive graph. Search, filter, zoom, and fit the graph to focus on what matters. Select an entry and open its connections without switching the document active in Etch.

### Built into your Etch workflow

Open Content Explorer from the compass in the upper sidebar, or go straight to the current item's connections with its dedicated shortcut. Return to your list with one click. The controls follow Etch's sidebar styling and active states.

Customize sorting, columns, button appearance, hierarchy indicators, and status colors. Transfer your saved site settings between installations with a validated JSON export and import.

Content Explorer for Etch 2.0 is an upgrade for existing Etch Content Hub Sort installations. Your plugin folder and saved settings stay in place.

Maps reflect saved content and supported relationships. Dynamic links, runtime query results, and unsaved editor changes are outside the map index.
